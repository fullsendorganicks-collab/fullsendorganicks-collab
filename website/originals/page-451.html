The 6 Distortion Signals

What CDAI catches before your dashboard does.

Standard reporting tools show you what happened. These six signals show you what's about to happen — weeks before it appears in any existing tool. According to Google Ads and Meta's own documentation, platform-reported costs represent only the media spend — not the full cost of a lead acquisition.

Signal 01 CPL Inflation Platform-reported cost per lead is rising while true all-in cost is rising faster. The gap between what your dashboard shows and what you're actually paying is widening. Signal 02 Lead Quality Decay Close rates are declining on a rolling window. The leads are arriving but converting less. Cost per acquired customer is rising even when CPL looks stable. Signal 03 Fraud Concentration Anomalous traffic patterns consistent with click fraud, lead stuffing, or bot activity. Isolated before they dilute the broader campaign data. Signal 04 Chargeback Spike Chargeback rate is rising above baseline on a specific campaign or partner source. Financial exposure is building before it hits your accounting. Signal 05 Margin Compression Contribution margin is shrinking as a percentage of revenue, even when volume is holding. Hidden cost layers are eating the margin your dashboard doesn't show. Signal 06 Payout Bleed Partner or broker payout rates are creating a structural drag on margin. The economics of a partner relationship no longer work at current volumes or rates.
