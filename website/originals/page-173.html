PRIVACY & COMPLIANCE

Data Deletion Request

Allocera Intelligence respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. If you would like to request deletion of your data from our systems, please follow the instructions below.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your personal data, please send an email to:

alloceraintelligence@gmail.com

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Your full name
  • Email address associated with your account
  • Phone number (if applicable)
  • Company name (if applicable)
  • Description of the data you would like deleted

Processing Timeline

We will respond to your data deletion request within 30 days of receipt. Once processed, we will send you a confirmation email.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon request, we will delete:

  • Contact information (name, email, phone)
  • Company and business information
  • Campaign and marketing data
  • Any other personal information in our systems
Note: Some data may be retained for legal or regulatory compliance purposes as required by law.
