About CDAI Engine

The CDAI Engine was built to solve a problem nobody else was solving.

CDAI Engine is a deterministic cost reconciliation engine — not an AI. It connects to your ad platforms, CRM, and lead distribution stack, calculates your true cost per lead after every hidden fee and charge, and issues one enforceable directive per campaign every morning. Built by Nick Baum. See what it found on day one or review pricing.

The Founder

The CDAI Engine was built by someone who lived the problem firsthand.

Nick Baum is the founder of Allocera Intelligence and the builder of CDAI Engine. His background is in performance marketing, paid acquisition, and full-funnel measurement — owning GA4 and GTM architectures, Google and Meta campaign management, and conversion tracking across live, revenue-generating operations in regulated environments.

Working directly inside lead generation operations, Nick encountered the same structural problem consistently: platforms report only the costs they control. Broker fees, post-conversion refunds, chargebacks, compliance costs, and variable overhead never appear in campaign reporting — which means the cost-per-lead number used to make scaling decisions is systematically wrong.

The gap between what dashboards show and what campaigns actually cost is not a rounding error. At meaningful spend levels, it is a capital allocation problem that compounds every single month. CDAI Engine was built to solve that problem specifically — and nothing else.
