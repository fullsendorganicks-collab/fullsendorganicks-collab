Common Questions

What people ask before they start.

What is the paid ads audit pricing and how is scope verified?
Scope is confirmed within 5 business days of platform access. We review actual campaign count and verified monthly spend through platform APIs. If you are borderline, we confirm the exact tier in writing before any work begins. You never pay for a tier that doesn’t match your actual data.
Can I start with the free audit before committing to a paid tier?
Yes. The free distortion audit runs on one campaign, 30-day lookback. It costs nothing and requires no commitment. It shows you exactly what CDAI finds before you decide whether to go further.
What does the audit credit actually mean?
If you pay $2,500 for a Quick Audit and start a Starter Retainer within 30 days, the $2,500 applies to your first 90 days of retainer. The credit is applied automatically — no negotiation needed.
Do I need to sign anything before data is shared?
Yes. An NDA and Service Agreement are required before any data access begins. This protects your campaign data before anything is shared. Both documents are straightforward and typically signed within 24 hours.
Not AI
CDAI is not an AI. It is a deterministic cost reconciliation engine — it performs math on verified data. There is no prediction, no model inference, and no hallucination. What it produces is a calculation based on what actually left your business.
