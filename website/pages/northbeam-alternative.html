What Northbeam Does Well

Before choosing a Northbeam alternative, it helps to be clear about what Northbeam is good at. Northbeam calls itself “the marketing intelligence platform for profitable” growth, with a full suite of measurement: multi-touch attribution, incrementality, and media mix modeling.

  • Multi-touch attribution shows which touchpoints across channels contributed to each conversion.
  • Media mix modeling estimates how each channel contributes over time, including channels that are hard to track click by click.
  • Incrementality testing. Northbeam says its incrementality product automates lift testing and unifies the results with its attribution and mix modeling. AdExchanger reported in April 2026 that it launched with Meta and Google as test platforms.

Independent reviews, such as SalesHive’s Northbeam review, describe it as built for ecommerce and direct-to-consumer brands. If that is your business, Northbeam is a serious measurement platform, and you may only need a Northbeam alternative for reasons like budget or team fit.

Signs You Need a Northbeam Alternative (or a Different Kind of Tool)

Most searches for a Northbeam alternative fall into two groups. Ecommerce brands usually want similar attribution at a different price or with a different interface. Lead generation and service businesses usually have a different problem entirely. You are in the second group if these sound familiar:

  1. Your sale closes in a CRM, not a checkout. A signed case, a booked job, or a new resident is recorded in HubSpot or Salesforce, often weeks after the lead.
  2. You buy leads or pay partners. Lead vendors and partners take a payout on every lead or deal, and it varies by partner.
  3. Money comes back later. Refunds, cancellations, and chargebacks arrive long after the original conversion.
  4. You need a decision, not another dashboard. Your team wants to know what to do with each campaign tomorrow, not interpret a new set of charts.

If that is you, switching from one attribution tool to another won’t close the gap. The Northbeam alternative you need is a profit view that sits on top of the tools you already use.

What to Look for in a Northbeam Alternative for Lead Generation

If you sell through leads, judge any Northbeam alternative against the way your money actually moves. These six questions separate tools that fit lead generation from tools built for store checkouts.

1. Does it read revenue from your CRM?

Your real revenue is the signed case, the booked job, or the move-in recorded in HubSpot or Salesforce. A tool that only sees a conversion pixel or an ad platform’s reported value is measuring a proxy, not the money you kept.

2. Can it wait for deals to close?

Lead generation sales cycles run days to months. A tool that judges a campaign after a week will cut campaigns whose deals simply haven’t closed yet. Look for a rolling window you can match to your sales cycle.

3. Does it count partner payouts per campaign?

If you buy leads, the partner payout can be the largest cost after ad spend. It has to be tied to the campaign and the partner that produced each lead, not spread evenly across the month.

4. Does it tie refunds and chargebacks back to the original campaign?

Money that comes back weeks later should reduce the profit of the campaign that produced the sale, not whichever month it lands in. Otherwise your worst campaigns look fine and your best look worse.

5. Does it tell you what to do?

Measurement is only useful if someone acts on it. Ask whether the tool gives a clear recommendation for each campaign, with the numbers behind it, or leaves your team to interpret charts. ROAS alone is not a decision.

6. Does it refuse to act on bad data?

Broken tracking and stale feeds happen. A good Northbeam alternative for lead generation should flag missing or suspicious data and hold its recommendations until the data is fixed, instead of confidently telling you to scale a campaign on numbers that are wrong.

Here is an illustrative lead generation campaign that shows why a Northbeam alternative for lead generation has to measure profit, not just credit. The attribution view credits it with $150,000 in closed revenue on $50,000 of ad spend, a 3.0 ROAS. The credit is accurate. Now follow the money:

LineAmount
Closed revenue credited to the campaign$150,000
Refunds and chargebacks−$15,000
Revenue actually kept$135,000
Cost to deliver the work (50% of revenue kept)−$67,500
Ad spend−$50,000
Lead partner payouts−$20,000
Financing, payment, and compliance fees−$4,000
Contribution margin−$6,500 (−4.8%)

On an attribution dashboard, a 3.0 ROAS looks healthy. After every cost, this campaign loses $6,500 a month. Scaling it would make the loss bigger. The fix might be renegotiating the partner payout or cutting spend, and neither decision is visible from attribution credit alone. Northbeam Alternative FAQs

What is the best Northbeam alternative?

It depends on your business. Ecommerce brands usually compare other attribution tools such as Triple Whale, Rockerbox, and Polar Analytics. Lead generation and service businesses whose revenue closes in a CRM often need a profit layer like Allocera CDAI rather than another attribution model.

What does Northbeam do?

Northbeam is a marketing intelligence platform that combines multi-touch attribution, media mix modeling, and incrementality testing to show which channels and campaigns drive conversions.

Is Northbeam good for lead generation?

Northbeam is built mainly for ecommerce and direct-to-consumer brands. If your revenue closes in a CRM weeks after the lead and your biggest costs are partner payouts, refunds, and fees, check how any attribution tool handles that before you buy.

Does Northbeam offer incrementality testing?

Yes. Northbeam launched an incrementality product that automates lift testing and combines results with its attribution and mix modeling. AdExchanger reported in April 2026 that it started with Meta and Google as test platforms.

Is Allocera a replacement for Northbeam?

Not in the attribution sense. CDAI doesn’t assign credit to touchpoints. It calculates profit per campaign from your ad accounts and CRM and makes one decision per campaign each night. It can run alongside Northbeam; it doesn’t connect to Northbeam directly.

What is incrementality testing?

Incrementality testing checks whether a campaign or channel caused sales that would not have happened anyway, usually by comparing a group that saw the ads with a group that didn’t. It answers “did this ad cause the sale?” rather than “did this ad make money?”

What is contribution margin in marketing?

Contribution margin is the revenue a campaign actually kept, minus every variable cost of winning it: ad spend, partner payouts, the cost to deliver the work, and fees. It shows whether a campaign added profit or took it away, which ROAS and attribution credit can’t show on their own.

Why can a campaign with a good ROAS lose money?

ROAS divides revenue by ad spend and ignores everything else. Refunds, the cost to deliver the work, partner payouts, and fees can turn a 3.0 ROAS into a loss, as the example above shows.
