How It Works: Marketing Analytics Software That Shows What Every Campaign Made

Most marketing analytics software stops at the dashboard: clicks, leads, and which channel gets credit. CDAI (Capital, Decision, Accuracy, Intelligence), the engine behind Allocera Intelligence, goes one step further. It connects your ad accounts and CRM, works out what each campaign really made after every cost, and gives you one clear decision for every campaign, every night. Here is exactly how, in four steps.

The short version

  • Connect: Meta, Google Ads, LinkedIn, HubSpot, and Salesforce connect in one click. Other tools connect by API key, CSV upload, or webhook.
  • Calculate: every night, CDAI works out each campaign’s real profit: the revenue it closed minus every real cost of winning it.

Where Most Marketing Analytics Software Stops

Improvado describes marketing analytics tools as “software platforms that collect and unify marketing data from ad platforms, CRM, and web analytics into a single” view. HubSpot says its analytics help you “measure the performance of all your marketing campaigns in one place.” That is genuinely useful. Seeing every channel together beats logging in to five dashboards.

But for a business that runs on leads, the number that decides everything usually isn’t in any of those dashboards. A campaign’s leads may be bought through a partner who takes a payout. Some customers refund. Some payments are charged back weeks later. Compliance and fulfillment cost money on every sale. None of that lives in the ad account, so the dashboard can show a healthy return on a campaign that is losing money. Our guide to marketing costs walks through each cost the platforms can’t see.

CDAI was built for that gap. It’s marketing analytics software for lead generation that answers the question the owner actually asks: did this campaign make money, and what should I do with it tomorrow?

How CDAI Works in 4 Steps

  1. Connect. Link the ad accounts, CRM, and other tools you already use. Nothing about your campaigns has to change.
  2. Calculate. Every night, CDAI pulls fresh data and works out real profit, true cost per lead, and true customer acquisition cost for every campaign.
  3. Decide. For each campaign, it makes one clear call, with the numbers and a confidence level behind it.
  4. Grade. It rechecks its own math and, once enough time has passed, grades whether each past decision was right.

What CDAI Calculates

This is the part most marketing analytics software skips. For every campaign, every night, CDAI calculates real profit:

Real profit = revenue closed − ad spend − fees − partner payouts − refunds − chargebacks − compliance costs − operating costs you add From the same data it also works out True cost per lead · True customer acquisition cost · Margin per campaign

Here is what that looks like for one campaign. This is an illustrative example, not client data.

One campaign, one monthAmount
Revenue closed$30,000
Ad spend−$10,000
Partner payouts−$6,000
Fees−$500
Refunds and chargebacks−$3,500
Compliance costs−$400
Operating costs you add (fulfillment)−$6,000
Real profit$3,600

The ad dashboard would show a 3.0 return on ad spend ($30,000 ÷ $10,000) and call it a winner. The campaign did make money, but $3,600 on $30,000 of revenue is a very different story from what a 3.0 suggests, and a small rise in refunds would wipe it out. The Nightly Decision

Numbers alone don’t move budget, and that’s where most marketing analytics software leaves you on your own. So after calculating, CDAI makes one clear decision for each campaign:

Scale

The campaign is making real profit and the trend supports spending more.

Hold

It’s working, but not clearly enough to add budget yet.

Cut

Profit is thin or slipping; reduce spend before it turns into a loss.

Pause

The campaign is losing money after every cost.

Each decision comes with the numbers behind it and a confidence level. CDAI also watches for early warning signs, like true cost per lead rising faster than the dashboard’s, or close rates slipping, so problems show up before they reach your books. You stay in control of every move: CDAI recommends, and you decide what changes in your ad accounts. Our scale, hold, cut, pause framework explains how to use each call when setting budgets.

Marketing Analytics Software FAQs

What does marketing analytics software do?

It collects data from your ad platforms, CRM, and website so you can measure campaign performance in one place. Most tools focus on reporting and attribution. CDAI adds real profit per campaign and a clear decision for each one.

What are the four main types of marketing analytics?

Descriptive (what happened), diagnostic (why it happened), predictive (what will likely happen), and prescriptive (what to do next). Most dashboards are descriptive. CDAI is prescriptive: it tells you whether to scale, hold, cut, or pause each campaign, and shows why.

Does CDAI change my ads automatically?

No. CDAI makes a recommendation for each campaign with the numbers and a confidence level behind it. You decide what to change in your ad accounts.

What if a tool I use isn’t one of the built integrations?

You can upload a CSV export, which CDAI recognizes automatically for 9 common platforms, or send leads from any CRM or form tool through a webhook.

Do I need to connect everything before it works?

No. CDAI works with what you connect. Your ad accounts and CRM are the core; adding partner payouts, refunds, and other costs makes each campaign’s profit more complete.

How accurate are CDAI’s decisions?

In a controlled validation before release, CDAI’s decisions were 89.5% accurate overall (1,210 of 1,352 scored decisions) across 9 businesses in 7 industries, with the math matching 100% when checked two ways. The full results are in the validation report.
