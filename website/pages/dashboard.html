What a Marketing Dashboard Should Answer

Wrike describes a marketing dashboard as a reporting tool that pulls key metrics from multiple channels into a single, easy-to-read view. In practice, most teams build one in their CRM or a reporting tool, filtered to show things like “ad spend, ROI, clicks, form submissions, and traffic by source,” as one HubSpot community answer puts it.

That view is useful, but it answers “what happened in the ads?” The owner of a lead-driven business needs three different answers:

  1. Did each campaign make money after partner payouts, refunds, chargebacks, fees, and the cost of delivering the work?
  2. What does a lead really cost, compared with what the ad platform says?
  3. What should I do with each campaign tomorrow?

The CDAI marketing dashboard is built around those three questions. The numbers come from the CDAI engine, which calculates them every night from your connected ad accounts, CRM, and other tools. See how it works for the full process.

What’s on the CDAI Marketing Dashboard

Profit per campaign

Revenue closed, true cost, real profit, and margin for every campaign, so winners and losers separate at a glance.

Dashboard CPL vs. true CPL

What the ad platform reports per lead, next to what each lead really cost after every expense.

Tonight’s decisions

One call per campaign, such as scale, hold, cut, or pause, with the numbers and a confidence level behind it.

Early warning signs

Trends like close rates slipping or true cost per lead rising faster than the platform’s number.

Partner view

If you buy leads or calls from partners, margin by partner shows which relationships pay and which drag.

Decision history

Past decisions and how they were graded once the results were in. Our 30-day retest explains the grading.

Behind every view, CDAI rechecks its own math each night and stops on bad data instead of showing numbers it can’t stand behind. You stay in control: the dashboard recommends, and you decide what changes in your ad accounts.

How to Use the Marketing Dashboard Each Morning

  1. Start with the decisions. Look at anything marked cut or pause first; that’s where money is leaving.
  2. Check the CPL gap. A big difference between dashboard CPL and true CPL means costs outside the ad account are growing.
  3. Scale the proven winners. Add budget where real profit is strong and confidence is high.
  4. Watch the warning signs. A slipping close rate today becomes a losing campaign next month.

Marketing Dashboard FAQs

What is a marketing dashboard?

A marketing dashboard is a single view of your key marketing numbers from multiple channels. Most show clicks, leads, spend, and ROI. The CDAI dashboard adds real profit per campaign, true cost per lead, and one decision per campaign.

Can you give me an example of a marketing dashboard?

The example above shows four campaigns with the platform’s cost per lead, the true cost per lead, real profit over 30 days, and a scale, hold, cut, or pause decision with a confidence level.

What are the three types of dashboards?

Operational dashboards track day-to-day activity, strategic dashboards track high-level goals for leaders, and analytical dashboards help dig into why numbers changed. The CDAI dashboard is built for the daily decision: it combines the profit view leaders need with a clear call on each campaign.

How often does the CDAI dashboard update?

Every night. The engine pulls fresh data from your connected accounts, recalculates every campaign, and posts the new decisions before your day starts.

Does the dashboard change my ads?

No. It recommends a decision for each campaign. You make every change in your ad accounts.
