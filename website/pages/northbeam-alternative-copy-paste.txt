<section id="checklist">
  <div class="section-inner prose">
    <h2 class="section-title fade-up">What to Look for in <em>a Northbeam Alternative for Lead Generation</em></h2>
    <div class="rule fade-up"></div>
    <div class="fade-up"><p>If you sell through leads, judge any Northbeam alternative against the way your money actually moves. These six questions separate tools that fit lead generation from tools built for store checkouts.</p>
<h3>1. Does it read revenue from your CRM?</h3>
<p>Your real revenue is the signed case, the booked job, or the move-in recorded in HubSpot or Salesforce. A tool that only sees a conversion pixel or an ad platform&rsquo;s reported value is measuring a proxy, not the money you kept.</p>
<h3>2. Can it wait for deals to close?</h3>
<p>Lead generation sales cycles run days to months. A tool that judges a campaign after a week will cut campaigns whose deals simply haven&rsquo;t closed yet. Look for a rolling window you can match to your sales cycle.</p>
<h3>3. Does it count partner payouts per campaign?</h3>
<p>If you buy leads, the partner payout can be the largest cost after ad spend. It has to be tied to the campaign and the partner that produced each lead, not spread evenly across the month.</p>
<h3>4. Does it tie refunds and chargebacks back to the original campaign?</h3>
<p>Money that comes back weeks later should reduce the profit of the campaign that produced the sale, not whichever month it lands in. Otherwise your worst campaigns look fine and your best look worse.</p>
<h3>5. Does it tell you what to do?</h3>
<p>Measurement is only useful if someone acts on it. Ask whether the tool gives a clear recommendation for each campaign, with the numbers behind it, or leaves your team to interpret charts. <a href="https://www.hubspot.com/glossary/return-on-ad-spend" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ROAS</a> alone is not a decision.</p>
<h3>6. Does it refuse to act on bad data?</h3>
<p>Broken tracking and stale feeds happen. A good Northbeam alternative for lead generation should flag missing or suspicious data and hold its recommendations until the data is fixed, instead of confidently telling you to scale a campaign on numbers that are wrong.</p></div>
  </div>
</section>
