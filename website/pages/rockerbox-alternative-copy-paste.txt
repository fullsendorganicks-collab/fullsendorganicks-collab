<section id="rockerbox">
  <div class="section-inner prose">
    <h2 class="section-title fade-up">What Rockerbox <em>Does Well</em></h2>
    <div class="rule fade-up"></div>
    <div class="fade-up"><p>Any honest look at a Rockerbox alternative starts with what Rockerbox gets right. <a href="https://www.rockerbox.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Rockerbox says it</a> &ldquo;unifies attribution, MMM, and incrementality testing to drive smarter decisions.&rdquo; For large brands with many channels, that combination is its strength:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Custom attribution.</strong> Rockerbox says its <a href="https://www.rockerbox.com/multi-touch-attribution-software" target="_blank" rel="noopener">attribution software for enterprise brands</a> builds a custom model from each client&rsquo;s own data.</li>
  <li><strong>Mix modeling.</strong> Marketing mix modeling estimates each channel&rsquo;s contribution over time, including channels that are hard to track click by click.</li>
  <li><strong>Incrementality testing.</strong> Tests show whether a channel caused sales that wouldn&rsquo;t have happened anyway.</li>
</ul>
<p>In February 2025, <a href="https://doubleverify.com/company/newsroom/doubleverify-to-acquire-rockerbox-adding-outcome-measurement-and-attribution-capabilities-to-its-suite-of-performance-measurement-and-optimization-solutions" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DoubleVerify announced it would acquire Rockerbox</a> in a cash deal valued at $85 million, adding outcome measurement and attribution to its products.</p></div>

<section id="why" style="background:#071530!important;border-top:1px solid #102040;border-bottom:1px solid #102040">
  <div class="section-inner prose">
    <h2 class="section-title fade-up">Why Teams Look <em>for a Rockerbox Alternative</em></h2>
    <div class="rule fade-up"></div>
    <div class="fade-up"><p>Searching for a Rockerbox alternative usually comes down to fit. Rockerbox is built for enterprise brands with complex media plans. Three situations push teams to look elsewhere:</p>
<ol class="steps">
  <li><span><strong>Size and budget.</strong> A smaller team may want a simpler tool it can run without a dedicated analyst.</span></li>
  <li><span><strong>Business model.</strong> If your revenue is a signed case, a booked job, or a new resident recorded in HubSpot or Salesforce weeks after the lead, you need a tool built around CRM revenue.</span></li>
  <li><span><strong>The question itself.</strong> Attribution tells you which channels earned credit. If your real blind spot is which campaigns and lead partners actually make money, you need a different measurement, not a different attribution model.</span></li>
</ol>
<p>The third reason is the one most teams miss. It&rsquo;s worth understanding the four ways marketing gets measured before picking a Rockerbox alternative.</p></div>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="switch" style="background:#071530!important;border-top:1px solid #102040;border-bottom:1px solid #102040">
  <div class="section-inner prose">
    <h2 class="section-title fade-up">Five Questions to Answer <em>Before You Switch From Rockerbox</em></h2>
    <div class="rule fade-up"></div>
    <div class="fade-up"><p>Switching tools is expensive, so answer these before you commit to any Rockerbox alternative. They also make vendor demos far more useful.</p>
<h3>Where does our revenue actually get recorded?</h3>
<p>If it&rsquo;s store orders, most attribution tools will read it well. If it&rsquo;s deals in HubSpot or Salesforce that close weeks after the lead, make sure any tool you consider reads closed revenue from your CRM, not just conversion events from a pixel.</p>
<h3>Which costs come after the sale?</h3>
<p>List every cost that shows up after a customer converts: refunds, cancellations, chargebacks, partner payouts, financing fees, compliance costs. If those are large, the decision you need depends on profit, and credit alone won&rsquo;t get you there.</p>
<h3>Do we compare lead partners, or only channels?</h3>
<p>Many businesses buy leads from several partners at similar prices. As the example above shows, partners with identical credit can produce very different profit. If partner decisions matter to you, you need a tool that measures each partner separately.</p>
<h3>Who acts on the numbers, and how often?</h3>
<p>A monthly review by an analyst needs different output than a busy owner who wants to know what to change tomorrow. Choose a Rockerbox alternative whose output matches how your team actually makes decisions.</p>
<h3>Do we need to replace anything at all?</h3>
<p>Sometimes the answer is no. If your attribution works and your blind spot is profit, adding a profit layer alongside your current tool can be faster and cheaper than migrating to a new attribution platform.</p></div>

</section>
